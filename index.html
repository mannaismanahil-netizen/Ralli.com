THE JOURNEY

RALLI began in 2024 with a bold mission: turning forgotten materials into conscious design. We debuted our concept at the Innovation Spark 2024 competition, featuring five distinct garments crafted entirely from reclaimed fabrics alongside a statement dress built from recycled newspaper. Driven by a clear vision to redefine sustainable fashion, our presentation earned first place—igniting a commitment to take this movement even further.
